# Survey Builder (Django)

A simple web-based survey builder with drag-and-drop question management and live preview.

### Features
- Django 4.2
- Bootstrap 5 (light mode)
- jQuery UI for drag and drop
- Live question preview
- SQLite database
- Auto admin account (admin/admin123)
- Sample survey with preloaded questions
